# KUAS Photography Club: Participation and Photo Use
Updated: October 17, 2026

This website is operated by the Kyoto University of Advanced Science Photography Club. This guide brings together confirmed activity information and points to check about participation and photo use. It does not indicate approval or amendment of formal club regulations. Ask the organizers about institutional requirements or matters requiring a decision.

## 1. The club and participation

KUAS students, including international students, are welcome to visit, with or without photography experience. A smartphone is enough. The club has 74 members as of October 2026. Members plan photo outings together. Shared cameras and other equipment are still being prepared.

Staff, alumni and people outside KUAS should ask the organizers whether participation or formal membership is possible.

## 2. Meetings and communication

Regular meetings are on Fridays from 18:00, usually in S306 at Uzumasa Campus. The regular meeting on October 23, 2026 is cancelled for Ryubisai festival preparations. Check the [schedule](/en/schedule/) and confirm with the organizers before visiting, as meetings may be cancelled or moved.

Members receive routine notices through LINE or other communication chats introduced by the organizers. General inquiries go through [email or Instagram DM](/en/contact/). Member group invitation links are not published publicly.

For late arrival or early departure, arrange the meeting point and time with the organizers in advance. Tell them about an absence or cancellation as soon as you know, and check the individual activity notice.

## 3. Membership, costs and insurance

[A visit request](/en/join/) is separate from formal membership. Joining a LINE or other communication chat alone does not complete membership. The organizers will explain the required formal membership procedures.

Club dues are JPY 1,000 per semester. Transport, admission and other outing expenses are paid separately by each participant. Check individual activity costs before applying.

Ask the organizers how membership procedures, activity registration and insurance procedures or eligibility relate to each other. Do not assume that applying or joining a chat automatically provides insurance coverage; check before the activity. This guide does not guarantee coverage or its scope.

## 4. Safety and photography etiquette

Do not enter railway tracks, restricted areas or places where safety cannot be ensured. Check local and facility rules on photography, tripods and flash. Respect people, pedestrian access, local communities and nature. Check the weather, travel route, meeting point and time beforehand; consult the organizers about changes or hazards.

Ask for consent to photograph people, and check publication or sale separately. Consent to being photographed does not grant permission for every later use.

## 5. Photo use and publication

Distinguish taking a photograph, website publication, social media, exhibitions, sales, commissioned photography and personal use. Check the conditions of the photographer, people depicted and facilities for each purpose. Submitting a work for an exhibition or sale does not authorize website publication. The club does not thereby gain unrestricted permission to sell or reuse members' work.

Use only a name or display name approved for public credit. For reuse, editing or sale, [contact the club](/en/contact/). Photography requests, sponsorship and press inquiries are discussed individually, including availability, fees, deadlines and image usage conditions.

Use the same contact channels to discuss removal or visibility of material on club-managed websites and social accounts. Include the URL and your request. The club will review it and consider action within the channels it controls. It cannot promise complete removal of third-party reposts or all copies on the internet.

## 6. Personal information and concerns

Information provided through forms and inquiries is used to arrange visits or participation and respond to inquiries. Member lists, student IDs, private contact details, bank information, individual payment records, unpublished works and submission folders are not published publicly.

Organizer names, portraits and photo credits are published only where public consent has been confirmed. Contact the organizers by [email or Instagram DM](/en/contact/) about concerns or information handling.

## 7. This guide and earlier versions

The Japanese and English guides cover the same information. The 2025 versions are retained as historical material and are not presented as current formal regulations. Other language versions are temporarily unavailable on the website until they have been updated and checked. Ask the organizers about formal regulations and approval of amendments.
