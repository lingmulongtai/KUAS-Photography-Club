# Head scan used on the focal-length page

`head.glb` and `normal.jpg` are the "Infinite, 3D Head Scan" by Lee Perry-Smith,
as distributed with the three.js examples (`examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith/`).

- Original work: Infinite-Realities, https://www.ir-ltd.net/
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- Changes: none to the files; the site renders the scan as a white plaster bust.

The credit is also shown under the demo on `/learn/focal-length/`.
